Where Is Deomali?

Deomali Hills is located in the Koraput district of Odisha, India. Standing at about 1,672 metres, it is the highest mountain peak in Odisha. Surrounded by beautiful green hills, forests and clouds, Deomali is a paradise for nature lovers.

Best Time to Visit Deomali

October to February 🍃

These months offer pleasant weather, cool breezes and beautiful views of the hills. It is a wonderful time for sightseeing and exploring nature.

Early Morning 🌄

Visit early in the morning to enjoy the peaceful atmosphere, fresh air and beautiful sunrise views.

How to Reach Deomali

By Air ✈️

The nearest major airport is Jeypore Airport. Travellers can also explore flights to Visakhapatnam or Bhubaneswar and continue by road.

By Train 🚆

Koraput Railway Station is an important railway connection for visitors travelling to the region.

By Road 🚗

Deomali can be reached by road from destinations such as Koraput and Semiliguda.
